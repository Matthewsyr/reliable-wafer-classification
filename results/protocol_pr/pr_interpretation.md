# Precision–recall decision analysis

The geometry feature block improved Scratch ranking on this fixed development split. Mean Scratch one-vs-rest Average Precision rose from 0.260 to 0.569 on tune and from 0.319 to 0.560 on the previously examined audit partition. The matched CNN reference achieved 0.392 and 0.300, respectively. These comparisons reuse the same heldout wafers across three training seeds; seed repetitions do not increase the sample size. AP is the non-interpolated sum over recall increments, not trapezoidal PR area.

Precision ≥ 80% is a hypothetical operating requirement for illustrating trade-offs, not a factory-approved target or a confidence guarantee. Each fitted model received one threshold selected on tune alone: maximize Scratch true positives among nonempty rules meeting the exact precision constraint, then break ties by precision and threshold. All nine thresholds were frozen together before this analysis read audit scores. Audit had been examined in earlier development work and is therefore an exploratory fixed-rule recheck, not a new independent test.

For the 77-feature RF, the tune-selected thresholds retained 30.3–38.4% Scratch recall on tune. Applied unchanged to audit, they achieved 83.8–87.9% precision and 36.25–38.75% recall. However, these binary operating points do not establish an improved nine-class system. Relative to the same models' unmodified argmax decisions, the Scratch-priority rule lost 2, 6 and 4 true Scratch detections on audit and increased total normal-to-defect errors by 2, 1 and 3. Thus this threshold rule fails the earlier joint objective of preserving Scratch recall while avoiding increased normal false alarms. The RF geometry block under ordinary argmax showed the favorable paired point estimates; the added threshold rule did not.

Error reassignment also matters. For the CNN reference, suppressing normal-to-Scratch false alarms on audit reassigned 125, 4 and 209 previously Scratch-labeled normal wafers to other defect labels across the three seeds. Total normal false alarms fell by only 100, 4 and 31, while true Scratch detections fell by 37, 7 and 39. The full nine-class confusion matrices and transition counts are therefore reported beside binary PR curves.

An independent verification passed 14,317 checks covering all 18 probability records, source hashes and row order, exact tune threshold optimality, sklearn AP, binary operating grids, all 36 nine-class evaluations and decision transitions. A further 260 checks independently reproduced geometry means, paired transitions, guardrails and component-bootstrap summaries. These checks validate calculations and artifact consistency; they do not create independent evidence of industrial generalization. No official test images or predictions were rescored for this analysis.
