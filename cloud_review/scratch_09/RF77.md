# RF77 Scratch pseudo-labels by wafer size

No size band passed the prespecified teacher qualification rule. Zero pseudo-labels were admitted and no student CNN was trained. The analysis used known development labels to estimate teacher precision. The unlabeled pool has no ground truth for this calculation.

Size support counts normal **training** wafers with exactly the same ordered height and width. A support of zero still allows wafers of other classes at those dimensions. The table uses RF77 seed 42, a nine-class Scratch argmax and a raw Scratch vote of at least 0.50. Each ratio gives true scratches divided by accepted wafers.

| Size support in training normals | Design: true scratches | Design: hits/accepted | Confirmation: true scratches | Confirmation: hits/accepted |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 0 | 29 | 7/7 | 44 | 10/11 |
| 1–24 | 0 | 0/0 | 2 | 0/0 |
| 25 or more | 10 | 0/0 | 14 | 0/0 |

The seven design hits came from six lots. Their one-sided 95% Wilson lower precision bound was 72.12%. Eligibility required at least 12 accepted wafers, five lots, 90% precision and a lower bound of at least 75%. Higher thresholds further reduced coverage. The other two bands accepted no candidates even at 0.50, leaving precision undefined.

The confirmation partition contributed a descriptive 10/11 at the predefined 0.50 cutoff, with one normal wafer misclassified. No threshold qualified for formal confirmation. Using only the nine-way argmax in the zero-support band yielded 13/14 on design and 22/26 on confirmation. All four errors on confirmation were normal wafers.

Further evidence would require blind human labeling across additional lots, with separate samples above and below a frozen threshold and independent lots reserved for student evaluation.
