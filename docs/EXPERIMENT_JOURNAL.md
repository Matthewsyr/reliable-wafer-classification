# Experiment journal

This journal preserves hypotheses and negative results. Historical evaluation and post-test exploratory work are separate scopes.

| Experiment | Hypothesis | Method and controls | Actual evidence | Interpretation | Limitations |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Historical RF/CNN | Learned local filters may outperform spatial summaries on unseen lots. | 30 classical and 18 CNN development runs; matched historical partitions; frozen selection and original test. | RF/CNN lot Macro-F1 85.28%/84.73%; original test 44.87%/45.16%. | The compact CNN does not resolve the generalization gap. | Exact-map overlap, label conflicts, population shift; no established future-time claim. |
| Size / BatchNorm diagnosis | Undercovered normal geometry and fitted normalization statistics may contribute to false Scratch predictions. | Inspect saved errors, compare size coverage, perform explicitly diagnostic normalization intervention. | 70.60% of original-test normals have dimensions absent in development normals; calibration diagnostic sacrifices true Scratch recall. | Coverage and normalization are plausible contributors; improving normal false alarms alone is insufficient. | Observational associations; intervention is not a causal decomposition or new official score. |
| First development repair | Improve normal coverage and retraining behavior while retaining Scratch sensitivity. | Fixed development component split; training changes and saved selection rules. | Original joint repair criteria not met. | Keep as failure evidence. | No fresh independent confirmation. |
| Second joint repair | GroupNorm, augmentation, averaging and weighting can jointly preserve Scratch TP and reduce normal total errors across seeds/refit. | Fixed four-config screen; frozen offsets; three seeds; refit checks. | Selected screen passes; all three refit comparisons fail joint requirements. | Better normal-error stability alone is not a successful repair. | Bundled intervention cannot isolate each component's effect. |
| Geometry block | Explicit line organization adds useful discrimination beyond original features. | RF71 vs RF77; same split, hyperparameters, weights and seeds; six fits only; lineage-matched existing CNN. | Audit mean Scratch recall 32.08%→42.08%; normal total FP/1,000 12.35→7.17; joint point-estimate improvement in all seeds. | Promising exploratory block-level improvement. | PCA re-expresses existing moments; audit was already seen; 80 Scratch examples; no new refit or independent-test claim. |
| 80% precision rule | A validation-selected constraint provides an interpretable operating point. | Unique tune scores; nonempty positives; exact precision feasibility; freeze before audit export; full nine-class re-evaluation. | RF77 audit precision 83.78–87.88%, recall 36.25–38.75%; its argmax has more Scratch hits and fewer normal total errors in all seeds. | Useful decision illustration, rejected as a repair. | 80% is hypothetical; score/prevalence changes invalidate transfer; point estimates are not guarantees. |
